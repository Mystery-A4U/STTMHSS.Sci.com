Put your school photo in this folder, e.g.:
    assets/school/school.jpg

Then open js/config.js and make sure this line points to it:
    schoolImage: "assets/school/school.jpg",

Tips for best results:
- Use a wide, high-resolution landscape photo (1920px wide or more).
- Center the building / main subject - the site crops the photo with
  object-fit: cover so it always fills the full screen, on any monitor.
- JPG or PNG both work.
